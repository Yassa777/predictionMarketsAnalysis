# Facet 4 Participation Depth

Last updated: 2026-06-15

## Purpose

This facet measures whether market calibration varies with participation depth.
It builds compact CSV outputs that can be committed under `research/tables/facet4`
so readers can inspect the results without downloading the full raw trade
database.

## Analysis

Analysis:
[facet4_participation_depth.py](../src/analysis/comparison/facet4_participation_depth.py)

Run:

```bash
uv run main.py analyze facet4_participation_calibration_by_depth
```

The analysis starts from:

- `data/derived/facet1_enriched_market_dataset.parquet`

and scans raw trade parquet only to attach participation fields:

- Kalshi: trade count and summed contract count before market close
- Polymarket CTF: trade count, USD volume proxy, unique maker/taker wallets,
  and wallet-volume HHI before market close
- Polymarket legacy FPMM: trade count, USD volume proxy, unique trader wallets,
  and trader-volume HHI before market close

Polymarket trade rows in this local dataset have null raw timestamps, so the
analysis resolves trade times through `data/polymarket/blocks/*.parquet` before
filtering to trades at or before the market close timestamp.

## Output Files

Generated under `output/` and copied to `research/tables/facet4/`:

- `facet4_participation_market_features_part_001.csv` through
  `facet4_participation_market_features_part_004.csv`
- `facet4_participation_calibration_by_depth.csv`
- `facet4_polymarket_wallet_concentration.csv`
- `facet4_kalshi_trade_count_open_interest_controls.csv`

No chart output is generated by default. CSVs are the primary research artifact.

## Market Feature Table

The `facet4_participation_market_features_part_*.csv` files together keep one
row per resolved market from the Facet 1 enriched dataset and add participation
features. The table is split into GitHub-safe parts because the complete CSV is
larger than GitHub's per-file limit:

- shared calibration fields: `platform`, `market_id`, `close_ts`,
  `reference_price_cents`, `reference_won`, and 5-cent price bucket columns
- platform-native liquidity fields from Facet 1 enrichment
- `trade_count`
- `contract_or_usd_volume_proxy`
- `contract_or_usd_volume_proxy_unit`
- Polymarket wallet fields when available:
  `polymarket_unique_wallet_count`, `polymarket_wallet_hhi`,
  `polymarket_effective_wallet_count`
- Kalshi controls when available:
  `kalshi_trade_count`, `kalshi_contract_volume_from_trades`,
  `kalshi_volume_contracts`, `kalshi_open_interest_contracts`
- analysis bucket columns for participation depth, Polymarket HHI, Kalshi trade
  count, and Kalshi open interest

## Bucket Definitions

Participation depth uses platform-relative stable quintiles:

- Kalshi depth metric: `trade_count`
- Polymarket depth metric: `polymarket_unique_wallet_count` when available,
  otherwise `polymarket_trade_count`

Polymarket wallet concentration uses stable quintiles of
`polymarket_wallet_hhi`, where higher HHI means more concentrated wallet-side
volume.

Kalshi controls use the cross of:

- stable quintiles of `trade_count`
- stable quintiles of `kalshi_open_interest_contracts`

The quintile assignment sorts by the target metric and `market_id`; ties may be
split across adjacent quintiles to keep buckets balanced.

## Calibration Metrics

Each summary table recomputes 5-cent price-bucket calibration inside the
requested participation slice, then reports:

- `market_count`
- `bucket_count`
- `expected_calibration_error`
- `signed_calibration_gap`
- `low_tail_gap`
- `high_tail_gap`

These definitions match the Facet 1 slice utilities.

## Runtime Notes

The feature build is intentionally DuckDB-first. The large scans are:

- `data/kalshi/trades/*.parquet` for Kalshi market trade counts
- `data/polymarket/trades/*.parquet` plus `data/polymarket/blocks/*.parquet`
  for current Polymarket wallet aggregation
- `data/polymarket/legacy_trades/*.parquet` plus block timestamps for legacy
  Polymarket wallet aggregation

The output is market-level and aggregated; it does not export raw trades or raw
wallet-level rows.
